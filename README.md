# Redesigning-Spotify-s-Listening-Insights
Redesigning Spotify’s Listening Insights: Improving Access to Personal Music Statistics Beyond Spotify Wrapped

# INTRODUCTION
Spotify is one of the most used music streaming applications in the world offering personalized songs recommendations, podcasts and audiobooks  to its users through an interactive mobile and desktop interface. In 2016, Spotify released the “Spotify Wrapped” which is an annual summary that presents to all users their insights and listening habits of the past year. It shows their most played artists, songs, albums, genres and their yearly time of listening. While Spotify Wrapped is highly engaging and AI was more integrated over the years, it only appears once a year providing users visual and  data driven personalized stories. 

Many Spotify users are curious about their listening habits throughout the year and are interested in their patterns and music preferences on demand instead of waiting the whole year for the Spotify Wrapped report. 
The objective of this project is to redesign the Spotify listening interface to allow users to have more interaction and accessibility to the way they can explore their personal listening data. Through user research, including tools such as surveys and interviews, the project will focus on identifying the frustration and needs related to the current system that users are facing. Based on these insights and data, alternative designs will be brainstormed and prototyped. After that, an user evaluation will be conducted to determine which design would be most effective on understanding the listening habits.

My motivation for choosing this project comes from both personal interest in music and interest in user experience design. As a big Spotify user, I liked the Wrapped concept when it first came up and I was excited every end of the year when Spotify would release it. But over the years, I realized that I would much enjoy it if it was more accessible the whole year. I even challenged myself once and asked for my spotify data and created a PowerBi dashboard with my mid-year data to see some of my trends. This project will offer the opportunity to explore the interface design, make it personal and enjoyable to all the users, and make sure that they have insights without it being overwhelming.
